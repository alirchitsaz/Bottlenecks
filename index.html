    FAQ

    Common Questions & Answers

    Got a specific question about your software setup? Schedule a call with our team or check out the answers below.

    How long does setup actually take?

    For Smart Scan, most setups go live within 5–7 business days from our 30-minute kickoff call. Data Sync takes about 2 weeks because we thoroughly test two-way syncing against your actual historical vendor bills. Full Back-Office workflows are scoped during discovery and typically deploy in 3–4 weeks.

    Can I speak with someone to confirm this works with my setup?

    Yes, absolutely! You can schedule a direct 30-minute video discovery call with our team on Calendly. We'll examine your current documents and tools and tell you honestly whether MagicFolder can automate them.

    What accounting and storage systems do you integrate with?

    Accounting: QuickBooks Online and Xero. Storage: Google Drive, Dropbox, Microsoft OneDrive, Box, and on-premise network shared drives. Email: Gmail, Google Workspace, Microsoft Outlook / Exchange. CRM: HubSpot, Pipedrive, and custom line-of-business tools. We add custom API connectors for roughly 85% of special customer requests at no additional charge.

    What happens if MagicFolder encounters a blurry photo or unusual document?

    We build in a simple Human-in-the-Loop Review Queue. If confidence falls below 95% (e.g. a crumpled receipt or a new supplier with no prior history), the document is routed to a clean "Needs Review" folder with suggested values highlighted. You review it in 15 seconds with a single click. We also monitor your system actively for the first 60 days to continually improve accuracy.

    Will this AI hallucinate numbers or invent supplier names?

    No. While our extraction engine uses modern multimodal models to read messy layouts, every extracted field (Vendor, Invoice #, Date, Amount, Line Items) is strictly reconciled against your pre-existing QuickBooks or Xero vendor database before any transaction is drafted. We never auto-create a new vendor without your explicit approval.

    What does the monthly software management fee cover?

    The monthly fee covers three critical pillars: (1) enterprise automation hosting and document processing infrastructure, (2) ongoing rule updates when your vendors or chart of accounts change, and (3) real human engineering support. We do not charge punitive per-document fees — file as much as you need.

    What is your deployment guarantee? What if I want to cancel?

    We believe in earning your business every month. There are no annual contracts — software management is month-to-month, and you can cancel anytime with 30 days' notice. During onboarding, we test your pipeline on 25–50 of your actual past documents until extraction accuracy exceeds 99%. Even if you ever cancel, the clean folder taxonomy, file naming rules, and accounting workflows we configure stay permanently in your accounts.

    Do you offer an offline, air-gapped on-premise version?

    Yes. For law firms, defense contractors (ITAR), medical practices (HIPAA), and CPAs requiring complete data isolation, we offer a dedicated on-premise edge server appliance that runs 100% offline on your local network with zero external cloud connectivity. Inquire via a discovery call.
